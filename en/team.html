Our Team

Behind every successful admission is a team giving their absolute best

Our Philosophy

We believe great education consulting isn't an assembly line — it's about truly understanding each student's unique qualities and using professional expertise with genuine care to help them make the best decisions. We don't chase volume; we chase quality. Every case deserves to be treated with full dedication.

Our Story

Founded in 2019, Shinka Education began when founder Jaden started helping students based on his own first-hand application experience while studying in Japan. Over the years, we've secured nearly 400 offers from top universities including UTokyo, Kyoto University, Waseda, as well as Oxford, University of Chicago, NUS, and HKU worldwide.

A Message from Our Founder

The reason I started Shinka Education is simple: I wanted every student aspiring to study in Japan to receive truly professional, sincere help — not to be treated as just another "signed contract."

In this industry, we've seen too much anxiety caused by information asymmetry. Students who chose the wrong agency and missed the school that was perfect for them. Students whose research proposals weren't good enough and lost their chance with their target professor. I don't want these regrets to keep happening.

That's why we insist on boutique service — our team isn't large, but every member is deeply expert in their area. We don't make false promises or over-package. We simply use our real capabilities and genuine care to help you navigate this journey well.

—— Jaden · Founder, Shinka Education
